## Goal

Separate evidence-backed risk factors (smoking, immunosuppression incl. HIV, long-term oral contraceptive use, multiple pregnancies, persistent high-risk HPV) from internet noise (diet myths, douching products marketed for "cervical health", etc.). Frame around: **screening + vaccination are the only two things with strong evidence**; everything else is secondary.
