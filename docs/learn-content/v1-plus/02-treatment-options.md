## Goal

Cover LLETZ (large loop excision), cone biopsy, and laser treatment. Explain what each does, recovery, and impact on future fertility / pregnancy. Be honest about the small-but-real risks (e.g., increased preterm birth risk after multiple LLETZ) without scaring readers off needed treatment.
