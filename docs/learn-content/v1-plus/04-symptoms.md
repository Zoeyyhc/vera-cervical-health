## Goal

Cover symptoms (abnormal bleeding  -  between periods, after sex, after menopause; unusual discharge; pelvic pain; pain during sex), but **strongly emphasise** that early-stage cervical cancer often has NO symptoms  -  which is exactly why screening matters. End with: "These symptoms have many other common causes; see your GP, don't self-diagnose."

**Note for writer (CLAUDE.md constraint):** This article must NOT generate diagnosis language. Always frame as "see your GP" and "these symptoms have many causes"  -  never imply that having symptoms = having cancer.
